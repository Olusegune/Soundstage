FFmpeg is included with Soundstage as a separate program (ffmpeg.exe). Soundstage starts it to write MP3, FLAC, AAC,
Opus, Ogg Vorbis and MP4 files. It is not linked into Soundstage.

Version:  FFmpeg n8.1 (release branch build 20261023)
Build:    BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds, "win64-lgpl" variant, configured with --enable-version3 and without --enable-gpl
          or --enable-nonfree, so it is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (see
          FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt).
Source:   FFmpeg source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html (tag n8.1)
          Build scripts and the exact configuration: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
          You may replace ffmpeg.exe with another build of FFmpeg: choose it on Soundstage's Export screen.
